# Fitting Ready — Garment & Appointment Planner

An independent MVP prepared for Twelve Tailoring in North Charleston, South Carolina.

Clients can organize their garment, event date, fit concerns, previous alteration history, appointment bring-list, garment handoff notes, preferred fitting sequence, and unresolved questions into one clean brief for the shop.

The app intentionally does not assess fit, take measurements, diagnose garment construction, recommend an alteration, quote, promise turnaround, confirm availability, schedule an appointment, accept a garment, approve rush service, collect payment, or place an order.

## Live site

https://trenn1x.github.io/specforge-analyst-studio/twelve-tailoring-fitting-ready/

## Implementation

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No dependencies, tracking, accounts, or data persistence.
