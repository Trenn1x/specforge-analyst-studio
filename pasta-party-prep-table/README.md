# Prep Table — Pasta Party Host Planner

An independent MVP prepared for Pasta Party CHS in Charleston, South Carolina.

Hosts can choose the closest public party lane, organize headcount and dates, check the published kitchen basics, flag setup and property access, record add-on interests and guest questions, map the event timing, and generate one clean host-and-kitchen conversation brief.

The app intentionally does not approve a kitchen or venue, assess food or allergy safety, set a menu, confirm staffing, calculate add-on or party pricing, accept a deposit, apply the cancellation policy, hold a date, confirm availability, submit the brief on its own, or book a party. Copying a brief transfers it to the user’s clipboard; opening an email draft transfers a compact version to the user’s mail app, but neither action sends the message.
