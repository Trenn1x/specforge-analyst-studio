01 · Current research

Progress on the Jacobian conjecture.

The plane Jacobian conjecture is still open. My current draft doesn’t pretend otherwise. The aim is to make the remaining obstruction easier to see, isolate, and test.

Independent draft · expert review wanted

Move the hard part to the boundary—then name exactly what is left.

I’m developing a boundary-at-infinity approach for planar Keller maps. Under the draft’s stated hypotheses, the bookkeeping condenses to a clean index relation. The characteristic-zero target then becomes concrete: rule out finite-value punctures at the boundary.

Separately, exact-arithmetic certificate computations check positive-characteristic families with growing generic degree. Those computations are evidence about the mechanism, not a shortcut to a characteristic-zero proof.
