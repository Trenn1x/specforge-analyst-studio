# Lowcountry Piano Visit Planner

An independent MVP concept for Lowcountry Piano Services in the Charleston area. It helps a prospective client describe their piano, surface a cautious service-conversation lane from the business's public descriptions, prepare photos and access details, and create an email-ready brief.

The planner intentionally does not diagnose a piano, select a binding service, confirm pricing or availability, book an appointment, promise repairs, or guarantee tuning stability. Jeremy remains the professional who confirms the right fit.
