# Scene Ready — Event Flow & Load-In Planner

An independent MVP prepared for The French Eclectic in Charleston, South Carolina.

The app complements the company’s existing wishlist flow. A host or planner can organize the event, rough scene dimensions and placement, intended rental pieces, venue access, setup and strike timing, visual direction, and open questions into one design-and-operations conversation brief.

The rough canvas supports pointer dragging and keyboard arrow movement. It intentionally does not read live inventory, reserve pieces, hold a date, calculate pricing or labor, guarantee fit or capacity, check accessibility or code, approve a venue or load-in route, confirm delivery or installation, upload photos, collect payment, submit anything on its own, or book an event.

## Implementation

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No dependencies, tracking, accounts, uploads, storage, or persistence. User-entered details remain in the browser unless the user copies the full brief or opens a compact email draft.
